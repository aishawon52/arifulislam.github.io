Assistant Engineer & Mechatronics / Edge-AI Researcher

I am currently an Assistant Engineer in the Operations Department at Chandpur 115MW Power Generations Limited, Bangladesh, where I work on IC engine operations, SCADA systems, and power plant automation. Alongside this, I am pursuing my M.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET), where I also completed my B.Sc. in the same field.

My research centers on sensor fusion, machine vision, and TinyML for embedded devices. My ongoing thesis explores non-destructive fruit ripeness detection through sensor fusion for intelligent harvesting decisions, building on earlier undergraduate work in handwriting recognition and pedestrian trajectory prediction using deep learning.

I'm also interested in applying Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and LLMs to industrial documentation, and in low-cost, hands-on hardware builds — from a handmade speed-controlled DC motor to an IoT-based classroom attendance system.
